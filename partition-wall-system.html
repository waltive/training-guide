Partition Wall System

Demountable, reusable interior walls — aluminum framework with any WALTIVE panel as click-in infill.

About Components Configurations Packaging Edge Trim Applications Talking Points
Home›Partition Wall System

What it is: A modular, demountable partition wall system that uses WALTIVE's existing SPC/WPC/Black PVC panels as infill, held in an aluminum/post framework (TNF224-15). The system includes vertical posts, horizontal rails, and connector profiles that allow panels to be slotted in without adhesive — creating reusable, reconfigurable interior walls.

Canton Fair Talking Points

  • Demountable & reusable — no glue, no demolition waste. Panels slide out and posts relocate. Huge selling point for exhibition and flexible office markets
  • Single post = 2-way — our unique single post can be placed horizontally OR vertically, giving designers more layout freedom than competitors
  • Uses our existing panels — customers already buying SPC/WPC boards can upgrade to partitions without switching suppliers
  • 700mm and 1200mm modules — match standard door and furniture dimensions; 1200mm is the most commercial
  • Fluted panel inserts — GS9608 or WZ146-2 can be slotted between flat boards for textured accent strips within the partition
  • Pricing strategy: quote as system (posts + rails + panels + trim) or à la carte. Posts are aluminum — priced separately from PVC infill

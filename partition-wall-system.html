Partition Wall System

14 Components · Modular, demountable partitions — SPC posts and rails with 50mm infill boards. Reusable, reconfigurable, fast to install.

About Components Pricing Packaging Applications Talking Points

What it is: A complete modular partition system (QS3005A series) using extruded SPC profiles — posts, rails, door frames — with a 50mm thick SPC infill board (313mm wide). A 30×30mm steel pipe inside each post provides structural rigidity. Designed for office cubicles, exhibition booths, retail displays, and temporary room dividers. All 14 components are reusable and demountable.

Canton Fair Talking Points

  • Complete 14-component system — QS3005A series covers boards, posts, tracks, door frames, bases, and steel reinforcement. One supplier, one shipment
  • Steel-reinforced posts — 30×30mm galvanized steel pipe inside every SPC post gives structural rigidity that pure PVC partitions can't match
  • Demountable & reusable — no glue, no demolition waste. Panels slide out and posts relocate. Ideal for exhibition booths and flexible offices
  • L-Post = 2-way — can be placed horizontally OR vertically, giving layout freedom. T-Post (3-way) and Cross Post (4-way) handle any junction
  • Transparent pricing — Board $16.92/sqm, posts $1.58–$3.09/m, bases $0.31/pc. No hidden "system pricing" — buyers can cost out any wall configuration
  • Door-ready — dedicated door frame (QS3005A-6MB), casing (7MT), and ceiling header (5) make door integration straightforward

SPC Wall Boards

24 SKUs · Flat stone-plastic composite panels — the volume driver of the WALTIVE portfolio, from 150mm strips to 1120mm wide boards.

About Specs Packaging Pricing Applications Talking Points
Home›SPC Wall Boards

What it is: Stone Plastic Composite (SPC) flat wall boards — calcium carbonate + PVC core, laminated with 0.12mm decorative PVC film. Waterproof, fire-rated Class B1, zero formaldehyde. Available in 6mm (lightweight) and 8mm (premium) thicknesses.

Canton Fair Talking Points

  • Lead-free formulation — our SPC uses no lead stabilizers, meeting EU REACH and French A+ standards
  • Width flexibility — from narrow 150mm strips to extra-wide 1120mm panels, reducing installation time
  • Four joint systems — Flat (F), V-groove (V), Puzzle (P), and Slotted (X) for different design aesthetics. NEW H8112P = 1120mm puzzle joint, premium wide board
  • 10 pcs/carton standard for narrow boards; palletized for FCL. 8PLT per 20' container on 1120mm wide
  • MOQ: Full container load (FCL). Mixed widths/colors allowed in one container

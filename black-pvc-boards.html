Canton Fair Talking Points

  • Solid black throughout — not surface-printed; cuts and edges stay black, no white core showing
  • Gold line option — decorative metallic inlay adds luxury feel for only +$0.08/pc
  • 2800mm length — slightly shorter than SPC (2900mm), optimized for standard ceiling heights
  • MOQ: Full container load — 10 pcs/carton, three-layer carton packing
  • Key differentiator: most competitors only offer black as a printed film on white core; ours is solid PVC

SPC Fluted / Grille Panels

29 SKUs · 16 Grille Panels + 13 Decorative Lines · 3D ribbed profiles that turn flat walls into textured features.

About Specs Packaging Pricing Applications Talking Points
Home›SPC Fluted / Grille Panels

What it is: 3D ribbed (fluted/grille) SPC panels that create textured accent walls. Two sub-families: GS-series Grille Panels (16 SKUs, 6–25mm thickness) and Decorative Lines (13 SKUs: H, S, SZ series, 18–110mm height). NEW for 2027: S18A01/02/03, SZ350-1, SZ150-1, SZ1411.

Key: Decorative lines have the highest CBM per carton (up to 0.16) because of their 30–110mm profile height. SZ350-1 is the widest line at 350mm ($2.23/pc). SZ1411 is the thickest at 110mm. Plan container loads carefully for heavy profiles.

Canton Fair Talking Points

  • GS-prefix = Grille SPC — the number after GS indicates width (e.g., GS150 = ~150mm wide)
  • Decorative Lines = H, S, SZ series — 13 SKUs total. NEW S18A (flat wide) and SZ (extra wide/thick) for 2027. SZ350-1 = widest at 350mm, $2.23
  • GS9025 is unique — 87×25mm, the deepest profile, creates dramatic shadow lines
  • All 2900mm length — consistent with wall boards for seamless installation
  • Pair with SPC wall boards — fluted panels as accents on flat board walls = complete solution

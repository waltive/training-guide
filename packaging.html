⚠️ Four Critical Packaging Differences

1

Pieces per carton varies wildly

From 2 pcs (SPC 1120mm board, H2008 line) to 40 pcs (small mouldings). Always check the specific SKU — never assume a standard count.

2

Only SPC boards have pallet specs

Wall boards list fumigation-free pallet counts (80–120 pcs/plt, 8–16 plt/20'FCL). Other categories don't specify palletization — offer to calculate per order.

3

Black PVC = three-layer cartons

Heavier, stronger carton than standard. Factor into shipping costs — three-layer cartons weigh more and take slightly more space.

4

Mouldings use cm, not mm

Carton dimensions quoted in centimeters (e.g., 292×17×11 cm). Same physical size as mm quotes but easy to misread — convert when calculating container loads.

Fair floor tip: When a customer asks "how many fit in a 20ft container?" — the answer depends entirely on category and SKU mix. Have a calculator or quick-reference sheet ready. A 20'FCL holds ~28–30 CBM. Divide total CBM by per-carton CBM to estimate cartons, then multiply by pcs/carton.

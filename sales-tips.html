Eight Strategies That Close Deals

Work through these in order — each one either opens a bigger conversation or removes a reason to say no.

1

Lead with the System

Don't sell individual panels — sell the complete wall solution. Flat boards + fluted accents + mouldings + partition framework. Customers who buy one category buy 3+.

2

Know Your Widths

SPC boards come in 150/200/300/400/600/900/1000/1120mm. The 600mm (H8600) is the workhorse. 1120mm (H8112) is the wide-panel premium upsell.

3

Joint Code Decoder

F = Flat seam, V = V-groove, P = Puzzle/lock seam, X = Slotted. Customers ask by look — know which code matches.

4

Gold Line Upsell

Black PVC with gold line (G-suffix) costs only +$0.08/pc but looks premium. Always show both samples — 60% of buyers choose gold line for retail/hospitality.

5

Partition = New Revenue

The partition wall system is brand new for 2027. Pitch to exhibition builders, office fit-out contractors, and retail display companies. Demountable = reusable = lower total cost.

6

Certifications Close Deals

CE certified, French A+ (indoor air quality), Class B1 fire rated, zero formaldehyde, lead-free. European buyers require these — have them ready in the first 30 seconds.

7

Mixed Container Friendly

Customers can mix SPC boards, fluted panels, and mouldings in one FCL. This is a big advantage over suppliers who force single-product containers. Mention it early.

8

Sample Kit Strategy

Offer a free sample kit with 3–5 representative pieces. The tactile feel of SPC vs WPC vs Black PVC sells itself. Always follow up with a formal quotation within 48 hours.

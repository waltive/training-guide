WPC Fluted / Grille Panels

Wood fiber warmth with PVC durability — solid and hollow grille profiles for premium interiors.

About Specs Packaging Pricing Applications Talking Points
Home›WPC Fluted / Grille Panels

What it is: Wood Plastic Composite (WPC) fluted panels — wood fiber + PVC core, giving a warmer, more natural wood feel than SPC. Two sub-families: GW-series Solid Grille (lighter, economical) and WZ-series Hollow Grille (larger profiles, hollow core for cost efficiency).

Canton Fair Talking Points

  • WPC vs SPC: WPC has wood fiber content → warmer, more natural texture; SPC is stone-based → harder, more dimensionally stable
  • GW = solid, WZ = hollow — hollow profiles reduce weight and cost while maintaining visual impact
  • WZ250B is the showstopper — 310mm wide × 25mm thick, the largest fluted panel, perfect for grand lobbies
  • MOQ: 1000 pieces / 300m per color — lower than wall boards, good for sample orders
  • R-prefix (GW1210R) = rounded/flame profile edge, distinct from square-edged standard

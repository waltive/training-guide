How to Use This Sheet

Start from the entry price when a buyer explores, anchor on the mid price while qualifying volume, and hold the flagship price for the premium SKUs (H8112F wide boards, WZ250B large fluted panels, gold-line black PVC). Every memory hook is phrased to be spoken aloud — rehearse them until the numbers are automatic. When prices change after the 30-day validity window, update each category page first, then mirror the change here so the cheat sheet always matches the detail pages.
